Taco Trivia

When did Tacos first appear in the United States?

Jiffrey M. Pilcher, taco historian and professor of history at the university of Minnesota, says the earliest mentions of tacos in the United States are in a newspaper from 1905. (Source: Smithsonion Magazine )
